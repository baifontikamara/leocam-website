Who We Are

Your Trusted
Construction Partner

LEOCAM — Sierra Leone Construction and Architectural Management — is a new-generation construction company based in Freetown. We provide excellent drafting, design and construction services, using the latest technologies and working closely with every client to deliver customised solutions that solve real constructional problems.

Our mission is simple: to build a better Sierra Leone by designing modern, standard buildings and constructing sustainable houses that last long.

Architecture & Engineering
